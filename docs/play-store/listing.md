## 2. English (Default)

### Short Description (74 / 80 characters)
Order school uniforms with instant AI size recommendations for your child.

### Full Description
Welcome to Colorbenz School Store — the modern, hassle-free way for parents and guardians to order tailored school uniforms, sports kits, and student essentials directly to their doorsteps.

Say goodbye to crowded tailoring queues and sizing guesswork. Designed specifically for busy parents, Colorbenz School Store pairs your school's official uniform catalogue with intelligent size recommendations, making school preparation seamless.

#### Key Features:
- **School-Specific Catalogues**: Select your child's school, grade, and house to see only approved uniforms, formal wear, sportswear, and accessories.
- **Instant AI Size Recommendation**: Enter your child's height and weight, and our verified sizing engine instantly recommends the right uniform size with clear shoulder, chest, and length measurements.
- **On-Device Camera Sizing Option**: Optional AI tape measure checks body dimensions directly on your phone — no photos or images are ever uploaded to any server.
- **Fast, Secure Checkout**: Integrated with Razorpay for instant UPI, Debit/Credit Card, and NetBanking payments.
- **Live Order Tracking**: Track tailoring, packaging, dispatch, and delivery milestones in real time with push notifications.
- **Easy Size Exchanges**: Simple 7-day exchange requests with automated size correction if your child undergoes a growth spurt.
- **Offline Mode**: View recently browsed uniform catalogues and keep your cart intact even without an active internet connection.
- **Biometric Security**: Unlock your account instantly and securely with fingerprint or facial authentication.

#### Note for Parents:
Colorbenz School Store is designed for parents, legal guardians, and schools. All accounts and purchases must be created and verified by an adult.

Need assistance? Contact our school liaison team at support@colorbenz.com.
