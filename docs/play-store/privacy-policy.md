# Privacy Policy: Colorbenz School Store

*Last Updated: November 17, 2026*  
*Effective Date: November 17, 2026*

Colorbenz Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Colorbenz", "we", "us", or "our") operates the **Colorbenz School Store** mobile application and web platform. We are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of all personal data entrusted to us by parents, guardians, students, and educational institutions in compliance with India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** and the **Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011**.

---

## 1. Scope & Target Audience

Colorbenz School Store is an e-commerce platform specifically designed for **parents, legal guardians, and school administrators** ("Users" or "Data Principals") to purchase school uniforms, athletic wear, and scholastic essentials for their enrolled children.

**We do not provide services directly to minors without parental consent.** All accounts, transactions, and student profile configurations must be initiated and managed by a parent or legal guardian.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only data necessary to fulfill school uniform orders and provide personalized size advisory services:

### A. Information Provided by Parents / Guardians:
1. **Account & Identity Data**: Parent's name, email address, mobile phone number, and account password (stored securely as a cryptographic hash).
2. **Student Profile Data**: Child's first name, school name, branch, grade/class, section, house color, and gender.
3. **Sizing Attributes**: Child's height (in centimeters or feet/inches), weight (in kilograms), and approximate shoulder width.
4. **Fulfillment & Billing Data**: Physical delivery address (shipping address, PIN code, landmark) and billing records.

### B. Device & Technical Information:
1. **Push Notification Tokens**: Device push token via Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver critical order lifecycle alerts (order confirmation, dispatch, delivery, and exchange updates).
2. **Device State & Network Diagnostics**: Operating system version, app version, network connectivity status, and basic crash telemetry.
3. **Biometric Data**: Fingerprint or facial recognition data is processed solely by the device's native operating system secure hardware (Android BiometricPrompt). **Biometric data never leaves your device and is never accessible to Colorbenz.**

### C. On-Device Camera Measurement Data (Strict Privacy Guarantee):
If you choose to use our optional camera-based sizing tool:
- The video stream is processed **100% locally on your smartphone** using Google MediaPipe.
- The app extracts geometric joint coordinates in temporary device RAM to calculate shoulder width.
- **NO camera photos, video frames, or facial biometric records are ever recorded to storage, uploaded, or transmitted to any server.**

---

## 3. Lawful Basis & Parental Consent

Under the DPDP Act, 2023:
1. Processing of student profile data and sizing attributes is conducted exclusively with **verifiable parental/guardian consent** provided during registration and student profile setup.
2. We do not engage in behavioral monitoring, targeted advertising, or profile-based advertising directed toward children.
3. All sizing data is utilized strictly to provide accurate uniform size recommendations and fulfill garment manufacturing and delivery.

---

## 4. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely for lawful business purposes:
- **Order Processing & Fulfillment**: Manufacturing, packing, and dispatching approved school uniforms.
- **Size Calibration & Advice**: Computing optimal garment fit to minimize returns and exchanges.
- **Customer Support**: Handling inquiries, exchanges, and warranty requests.
- **Service Communications**: Sending transactional receipts, dispatch updates, and delivery OTPs.

---

## 5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with:
1. **Logistics & Delivery Partners**: Shipping address and contact number are provided to trusted courier partners strictly to execute physical parcel delivery.
2. **Payment Gateways**: Payment transactions are securely routed through Razorpay (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified). We do not store credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers.
3. **Law Enforcement & Legal Compliance**: Only when formally required by Indian statutory authorities or court orders.

---

## 6. Data Security & Storage

- **Data Centers**: All customer and transactional data is stored in secure, encrypted cloud data centers located in India.
- **Encryption**: Data in transit is protected using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Cleartext HTTP traffic is strictly blocked on the mobile application.
- **Access Controls**: Multi-factor authentication and role-based access control (RBAC) restrict internal employee access.
- **Anonymization**: Retained sizing benchmark samples are systematically anonymized by permanently stripping parent names, student names, order numbers, and contact details.

---

## 7. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under Indian data protection laws, parents and guardians have the right to:
1. **Access & Review**: Inspect the personal data and student profiles stored in your account.
2. **Correction & Update**: Correct inaccurate or outdated details directly in the app.
3. **Erasure & Account Deletion**: Request complete erasure of your account, student profiles, and associated personal information by navigating to **Profile > Settings > Delete Account** or emailing `privacy@colorbenz.com`.
4. **Withdrawal of Consent**: Withdraw consent for promotional messages or optional push alerts at any time via device settings.
